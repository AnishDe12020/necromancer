1. A method for displaying a news feed in a social network environment, the method comprising: monitoring a plurality of activities in a social network environment; storing the plurality of activities in a database; generating a plurality of news items regarding the activities associated with a user of the social network environment; attaching an informational link associated with at least one of the activities to at least one of the news items, the informational link enabling a viewing user to participate in the same activity; limiting access to the plurality of news items to a predetermined set of viewing users; assigning an order to the plurality of news items; and displaying a news feed comprising two or more of the plurality of news items to at least one viewing user of the predetermined set of viewing users.

2. The method of claim 1, wherein the activities include adding a friend, commenting on a post, and joining a group.

3. The method of claim 1, wherein assigning the order is based on a relationship between the user and the viewing user.

16. A system for displaying a news feed in a social network environment, the system comprising: a monitoring module configured to monitor a plurality of activities in the social network environment; a database configured to store the plurality of activities; a news item generator configured to generate a plurality of news items regarding the activities associated with a user; a link component configured to attach an informational link to at least one of the news items enabling a viewing user to participate in the same activity; a privacy component configured to limit access to the plurality of news items to a predetermined set of viewing users and to assign an order to the news items; and a media generator configured to display a news feed comprising two or more of the plurality of news items to at least one viewing user.

17. The system of claim 16, wherein the privacy component applies privacy settings selected by the user.
